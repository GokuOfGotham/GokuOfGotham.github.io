Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026

VideoAI uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used by one channel owner to manage their own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data it accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

Only when the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, and only for the channels they choose:

  • Uploading and captions (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload the owner's videos and thumbnails, add caption tracks, add videos to playlists, and confirm which channel a sign-in belongs to.
  • Analytics, read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, countries, impressions and click-through rate of the owner's own videos.
  • Public data: search results and public statistics of public YouTube videos and channels, used to see which topics are popular.

How the data is used and stored

  • It is used only to publish the owner's videos and to write performance reports for the owner.
  • Sign-in tokens, the upload ledger and reports are stored only on the owner's own computers. They are not uploaded to any server run by the tool; there is none.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Reports and downloaded analytics files stay on the owner's computers, for the owner's own records, until the owner deletes them.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

This website and the tool do not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps and services, or via Google's security settings page. Local copies are deleted by removing the tool's sign-in files.
