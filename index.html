VideoAI uploader

VideoAI uploader is a private tool that one channel owner uses to publish and review videos on their own YouTube channels: American Airframe, Atleta, TheSpaceQ, The Glitchy Matrix, StormProof and Tactical Storm.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos, thumbnails and caption files to the owner's channels.
  • Reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics (views, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate) to review how videos perform.
  • Searches public YouTube videos to see which topics are popular.

It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. It only acts on channels its owner signs in to.

It uses the YouTube API Services. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
